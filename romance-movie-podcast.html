A loving roast of made for TV romance.

Cheese Louise is a romance movie comedy podcast from Nick and Jenny, a husband and wife duo in a small mountain town in Colorado. Craft cocktails, questionable plot decisions, and an unreasonable number of romantic misunderstandings are all welcome.

For people who enjoy the movies and question the logic

Hallmark, Lifetime, MarVista, and Reel One are part of the made for TV romance territory we are exploring. We love the familiar tropes, and the crazier they are, the more there is to talk about. You can root for the happy ending and still wonder who is covering that abandoned work deadline.

What to expect

Movie discussion, Cheesy Concoctions, games, and a Cheese Grating drawn from the movie's recorded tropes. We pick apart the plots and the peculiar rules that keep the Romantiverse turning. We are inviting you to join the movie night.
