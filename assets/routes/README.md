# Romantiverse route illustrations

These five matching illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool
on November 7, 2026, using the approved Bingo `meet-cute.webp` illustration as a
style reference. They are decorative; HTML supplies all route names and links.

The full 640px WebP images are paired with 320px variants for responsive loading.
The existing approved town banner, hosts' photograph, and Cheese Louise emblem
were preserved. `assets/cheese-louise-emblem.webp` is the existing Bingo center
emblem, copied without alteration.

## Art direction and prompt set

Use case: illustration-story. Square route artwork for Cheese Louise, a cozy
vintage romance-movie podcast. Match the reference's painterly storybook texture,
warm amber lamplight, and whimsical small-town atmosphere. Clear central subject,
cream background, breathing room. Cream, forest green, cranberry, muted coral and
warm gold. No text, letters, logos, watermarks, people or Christmas decorations.
No photorealism.

Subjects:

- `podcast.webp`: vintage gold broadcast microphone on a walnut desk, small
  cranberry heart detail, a cozy recording room and window light.
- `music.webp`: vintage walnut record player, black vinyl, muted coral sleeve
  with a tiny heart motif and a cozy record shop background.
- `bingo.webp`: cream five-by-five blank Bingo card with cranberry heart stamps,
  a brass bowl of wooden game markers and a movie-night table. Decorative only;
  this is not a playable card.
- `drink.webp`: copper mug of cranberry cocktail, lime wheel and ginger garnish
  on a wooden lounge table in amber lamplight.
- `community.webp`: forest green and cranberry armchairs, two tea cups and a
  shared cheese board in a welcoming small-town lounge.

## Shared styling

`cozy-vintage.css` owns the shared website styling. Its base colors and font
families match the public Bingo board: forest `#193f35`, cream `#f7f0e2`, paper
`#fffaf0`, cranberry `#873b4b`, coral `#e8a18b`, gold `#c49b52`; Playfair Display
headings and DM Sans body text. Website guides and the 404 page load the same
stylesheet. No game logic or private HQ data is included in this change.
