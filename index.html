So what is this place?

Welcome to the Romantiverse.

Cheese Louise is a husband-and-wife podcast about the wonderfully predictable rules, characters, careers, towns, misunderstandings, festivals, and third acts of made-for-TV romance movies.

Every episode, Nick and Jenny watch another movie, test the Rules of the Romantiverse, make a cocktail, and determine just how much cheese one story can hold.
