    So what is this place?

    Welcome to the Romantiverse.

    Cheese Louise is a husband-and-wife podcast about the wonderfully predictable tropes, characters, careers, towns, misunderstandings, festivals, and third acts of made-for-TV romance movies.

    Every episode, Nick and Jenny watch another movie, spot the tropes of the Romantiverse, make a cocktail, and determine just how much cheese one story can hold.
